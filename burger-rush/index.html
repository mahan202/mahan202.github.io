Burger Rush: Diner Tycoon

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to move your chef. Grab burgers from the grill, put them on the counter and serve the guests at the register. Collect the cash and stand on the price tiles to build tables, grills, a drive-thru, a patio, your own burger factory and a delivery depot. Hire crew and managers to run it all.

Support

Bought "Remove ads" and reinstalled the game or got a new phone? Open Settings (⚙️) in the game and tap "Restore purchases" (same Apple ID or Google account). Questions, bugs or ideas: mahanalqaisi@gmail.com.
