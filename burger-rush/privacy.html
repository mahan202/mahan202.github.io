Privacy Policy

Burger Rush: Diner Tycoon · Developer: Mahan AL Qaisi · Last updated: November 25, 2026

No account, no personal data collected by us. The game works offline. Your progress (money, level, what you built, your crew, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. We do not run servers, analytics or accounts, and we never sell personal data.

Ads. The game shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads you can choose to watch (for example for a boost, a bonus or to double a reward), full-screen ads between play, and a short ad when you open the game or come back to it after a break. To serve, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device: the advertising identifier (on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; on Android the Android advertising ID), IP address and coarse location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash/performance data. Ads in this app are limited to content suitable for general audiences. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices. On iPhone, you can allow or deny tracking in the iOS prompt and later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the phone). If you are in a region where Google asks for consent (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" in the game's settings (⚙️). Denying tracking or consent does not limit the game.

Purchases. The optional "Remove ads" purchase (a one-time purchase) is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) on iPhone or Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see or store your payment details; the game only learns whether you own it, to stop the full-screen ads. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact. We will update this page if anything changes. Questions or a request about your data: mahanalqaisi@gmail.com.
