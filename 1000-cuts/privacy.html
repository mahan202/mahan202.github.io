Privacy Policy

1000 Cuts: ASMR Slicing Game · Developer: Mahan AL Qaisi · Last updated: November 16, 2026

No account, no personal data collected by us. The game works offline. Your progress (total cuts, best scores, unlocked objects, sound setting) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. We do not run servers, analytics or accounts, and we never sell personal data.

Ads. The game shows ads from Google AdMob (a rewarded ad you can choose to watch to unlock an object, and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds). To serve, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device: the advertising identifier (on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; on Android the Android advertising ID), IP address and coarse location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash/performance data. Ads in this app are limited to content suitable for general audiences. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices. On iPhone, you can allow or deny tracking in the iOS prompt and later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the phone). If you are in a region where Google asks for consent (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" in the game's info sheet. Denying tracking or consent does not limit the game.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact. We will update this page if anything changes. Questions or a request about your data: mahanalqaisi@gmail.com.
