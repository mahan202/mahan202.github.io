Privacy Policy

Sky Squad: Skydive Battle · Developer: Mahan AL Qaisi · Last updated: November 24, 2026

No account, no personal data collected by us. The game works offline. Your progress (level, coins, stars, upgrades, sound setting) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. We do not run servers, analytics or accounts, and we never sell personal data.

Ads. The game shows ads from Google AdMob (rewarded ads you can choose to watch for extra coins, a boost, a revive or a skin, full-screen ads between levels, and a full-screen ad when you come back to the app). To serve, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device: the advertising identifier (on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; on Android the Android advertising ID), IP address and coarse location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash/performance data. Ads in this app are limited to content rated PG or lower (suitable for general audiences and teens). See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads). "Remove Ads" is an optional one-time purchase that turns off the full-screen ads; the reward videos you choose to watch stay available. The payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google Play on Android, under their terms: we never see or store your name, payment details, Apple ID or Google account. To unlock the purchase, and when you tap "Restore Purchase", the game asks the App Store or Google Play on your device whether this purchase belongs to your account; that answer is stored only on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. On iPhone, you can allow or deny tracking in the iOS prompt and later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the phone). If you are in a region where Google asks for consent (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), Google shows its consent form when the game first loads ads. Denying tracking or consent does not limit the game.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact. We will update this page if anything changes. Questions or a request about your data: mahanalqaisi@gmail.com.
