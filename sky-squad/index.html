Sky Squad: Skydive Battle

Free on the App Store

How to play

Tap JUMP and drag left or right to steer your squad of skydivers. Fly through the gate that grows your squad, dodge birds, helicopters and other obstacles, then open the parachutes and beat the enemy squad on the ground. Earn coins for upgrades. 100 levels with a boss every 5th level.
